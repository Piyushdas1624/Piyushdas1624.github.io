📱 How to Receive on Your Devices

Follow these steps once on your phone or PC to receive instant notifications whenever a note is dropped from anywhere.

1. On your Android / iOS Phone (Instant Push with sound)

1. Install the free, open-source ntfy app from Google Play or Apple App Store.
2. Tap + (Subscribe) and enter topic name: piyush-drop-1624
3. Done! Now whenever you drop a note from any random computer, your phone instantly buzzes with the message and a "Copy" button.

2. In Any Browser (Web Notifications)

Visit https://ntfy.sh/piyush-drop-1624 in Chrome/Brave/Edge and click Subscribe.
